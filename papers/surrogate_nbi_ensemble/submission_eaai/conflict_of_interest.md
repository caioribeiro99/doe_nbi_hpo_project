# Declaration of competing interest

The authors declare that they have no known competing financial interests or personal relationships that could have
appeared to influence the work reported in this paper.

**Disclosure of related prior work by the authors.** This manuscript builds on, and is explicitly framed as a
stress test of, a methodological framework developed in part by the present author team:

- Pereira, M. C., Tertuliano Ribeiro, C., Mendes, R. R. A., Campos, P. H. S., de Paiva, A. P. (2025). A hybrid
  multivariate normal boundary intersection approach with post-optimization assisted by mixture design of
  experiments. *Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence*, 162, 112510.
  DOI 10.1016/j.engappai.2025.112510. **Published in the journal to which this manuscript is submitted.**
- Ribeiro, C. T. (2026). Master's dissertation, Universidade Federal de Itajuba. Open access, no DOI.

The relationship is stated in the abstract, the introduction, a dedicated related-work subsection and the
contribution list. The manuscript claims no part of the DoE--RSM--NBI construction as new. A full dimension-by-
dimension self-overlap analysis is provided as a supplementary document.

The editor may wish to note that one configuration studied here (NBI-A: surrogate objectives with surrogate-derived
anchors) is the construction used in that prior work, and that this manuscript reports it failing on two of four
datasets. The authors consider this the appropriate way to follow up their own method.
