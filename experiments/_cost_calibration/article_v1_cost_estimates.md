## Recommendation

Calibrated worst-case `avg_seconds_per_fit = 0.135 s` (CatBoost on this Mac).
At a realistic 4× inflation (≈ 0.54 s/fit) for full DoE/CV under heavy
configurations, the projected wall-clock for the **headline 12×3×10**
scenario is:

| Profile | Wall-days |
|---|---:|
| Caio Mac while working | **7.7 d** |
| Caio Mac overnight only | **3.5 d** |
| Dedicated Mac (eff 0.70) | **1.2 d** |
| Combined two Macs (16w × 24h, eff 0.70) | **0.8 d** |
| Cloud 32w @ $0.10/h (eff 0.85) | **7.5 h, $24** |

Even the pessimistic 8× sweep ($\approx 1.08$ s/fit) finishes in
**2.4 days on a dedicated Mac** or **$48 cloud**.

### Verdict — keep the headline scope

**Run the full 12 datasets × 3 algorithms × 10 replicas.** A 5-replica
fallback is unnecessary at this calibration. If the per-dataset
real-world inflation turns out higher than 8×, drop to 5 replicas
*selectively* (only on the heaviest datasets) rather than across the
panel.

### Multiclass blocker — Dry Bean

`src/doe_xgb/metrics.py::compute_binary_metrics` calls
`precision_score` / `recall_score` with their default `average="binary"`
and uses a fixed `labels=[0,1]` confusion matrix. This is a
**hard blocker for Dry Bean** (multiclass, 7 classes) entering the
**headline tables** of v1.

Recommendation: **Option B.** Keep Dry Bean in the registry but defer
it to a *secondary multiclass stress test* in the v1 paper (or v1.1).
Concretely:

1. v1 headline tables use the **11 binary datasets** of the panel.
2. Dry Bean is reported in an **appendix / supplementary section** as
   a multiclass case study, contingent on a follow-up commit that
   adds `compute_multiclass_metrics(y_true, y_pred, y_prob)` returning
   `{f1_macro, balanced_accuracy, mcc, roc_auc_ovr, brier_multiclass,
   ece_multiclass}` and threading the new keys through
   `factor_analysis` so the FA / NBI pipeline accepts the multiclass
   responses.
3. Until that follow-up commit lands, the v1 orchestrator must
   refuse to run Dry Bean and emit a clear error pointing at this
   document.

We do **not** remove Dry Bean silently from the panel.
