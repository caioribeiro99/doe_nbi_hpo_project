## Recommendation

At the realistic 4x inflation, the headline 12x3x10 panel projects to ~1.23 days on a dedicated Mac and ~0.32 days / $24.38 on a 32-worker $0.10/h cloud. 30 replicas at the same 4x inflation multiplies CPU-hours to ~621.6h, dedicated-Mac wall days ~3.7. The 82-dataset doctoral panel at 10 replicas projects to ~8.43 days on a dedicated Mac (~2.17 cloud days, $166.58) under the same per-pair-mean assumption; do not start it locally past 1 replica. Recommendation: run the full 12x3x10 article v1 locally on the dedicated Mac. Reserve 30 replicas for *selected* datasets, not the panel. Run the doctoral 82-dataset benchmark at 1 replica locally first as a sizing check before scaling out to cloud.
